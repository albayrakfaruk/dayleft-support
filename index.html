How to cancel a subscription

Subscriptions and free trials are managed by Apple, not inside DayLeft billing. Cancel anytime in Apple Settings (you keep access until the end of the current period).

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone
  2. Tap your name → Subscriptions
  3. Select DayLeft and tap Cancel Subscription

Or manage subscriptions here: apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions

Billing & refunds: support.apple.com/billing

Restore purchases

If you reinstalled the app or switched devices, open Settings in DayLeft and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed into the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Delete your data

Your budget and expenses live on your device. We do not keep a cloud copy of your spending ledger.

  • In the app: open Settings and use Delete local data (or equivalent) if available.
  • Or delete the app from your iPhone — this removes local app data (device backups may still retain older copies until those backups are updated).

Canceling a subscription does not automatically delete on-device data.

Troubleshooting

  • Force-quit and reopen the app
  • Update to the latest version from the App Store
  • Restart your iPhone
  • Confirm notifications are allowed in iOS Settings → DayLeft if reminders are missing
  • For widgets: add the widget from the Home Screen / Lock Screen gallery, then open the app once so timelines refresh

FAQ

Does DayLeft connect to my bank?

No. There is no bank linking. You log expenses yourself. Your ledger stays on your device.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Use Apple Subscriptions (Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions). See the cancel section above.

I paid but Pro features are locked.

Use Restore Purchases in Settings, confirm the correct Apple ID, and wait a moment for entitlement sync. Still stuck? Email us.

Widgets show a blank or outdated number.

Open the main app so data reloads, remove and re-add the widget, and ensure you completed onboarding with a budget set.

How do I turn off daily reminders?

Disable the reminder in app Settings, or revoke notification permission in iOS Settings → DayLeft.

How do I permanently delete my data?

Use in-app delete (if available) or delete the app. See “Delete your data” above.

Is this financial advice?

No. DayLeft is a personal budgeting tool. It does not provide financial, tax, or investment advice.
